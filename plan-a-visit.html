      Plan a visit to Pauhari Baba Temple

      Everything you need to know before you come: timings, aarti, how to reach us and a few guidelines for a peaceful visit.

      Visiting hours

      The temple is open to all devotees every day, from 6 AM to 8 PM.

      Visiting in a group?

      Let us know before you come, so we can welcome everyone properly.

      • Send us a message on WhatsApp with your group size and the date you plan to visit.
      • We will reply to confirm as soon as we can.
      • Please keep the group together and follow the visitor guidelines below.

      Directions

      The temple is in Saraiya Ratnave, Amari 276142, Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh.

      By road

      Saraiya Ratnave is in Azamgarh district. Use the map for turn-by-turn directions. Local taxis, autos and private vehicles can reach the temple.

      By train

      Azamgarh Railway Station is the main railway station of the district. From there you can continue to the temple by taxi or auto.

      By air

      Check current flight options to the Azamgarh region (Varanasi is a major airport nearby), then continue by road or train.

      General information

      A few simple guidelines to keep the temple peaceful for everyone.

      Wearing of shoes is not permitted inside the temple.

      Wheelchairs are available at the main gate on a first come first serve basis.

      This is a smoke-free facility.

      Washrooms & drinking water facilities are available.

      Food and drinks are not allowed on the premises.

      Large baggage is not allowed inside the premises.
